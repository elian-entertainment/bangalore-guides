Section 1 · Alcoholic Drinks

Top 10 Alcoholic Drinks

Beer-led — the 11 house brews are the only independently verifiable drinks at Shakesbierre
  1. 1
    Beer Sampler Flight
    Six pours of every house brew (~150 ml each) — the single most praised drinking experience at Shakesbierre across all reviews. Start here; pick a favourite, order a pint.
    Dec 2025 TripAdvisor · 8+ reviewers mention sampler likely current
  2. 2
    Ale Othello — American IPA 5.7%
    Highest-rated house beer on Untappd; citrus-tropical hop profile. The brewpub's flagship. Order a full pint after the sampler if this one lands.
    Untappd 3.41 · 117 ratings likely current
  3. 3
    Weizenbock 7.1%
    Highest-rated seasonal brew; strong German wheat-bock — complex, malt-forward, warming. For anyone who wants depth over refreshment. Confirm it's on tap first.
    Untappd 3.39 · 33 ratings seasonal · confirm
  4. 4
    As You Spike It — Herb Saison 6.2%
    The most distinctive beer they brew — rosemary & thyme spiced farmhouse ale, fermented dry for maximum aroma. Nothing else on this list tastes remotely like it.
    Untappd 3.30 · 72 ratings likely current
  5. 5
    Brisky Brutus — American Stout 6.2%
    Dark, roasted — the best food-pairing beer in the lineup. Order with the lamb chops or a rich curry; roasted malt against fat is a time-tested match.
    Untappd 3.26 · 67 ratings likely current
  6. 6
    Romeo's Swagger — Belgian Witbier 4.7%
    Unfiltered wheat, low ABV, crisp and refreshing. The all-evening beer — the one you can nurse through the whole meal without getting heavy.
    Untappd 3.25 · 87 ratings likely current
  7. 7
    Taming of the Brew — Cream Ale 5.2%
    Smooth, malt-forward, gentle bitterness. The gateway beer for anyone in the group who doesn't usually drink craft — easy crowd-pleaser that won't challenge.
    Untappd 3.19 · 76 ratings likely current
  8. 8
    The Bard — Hefeweizen 5.1%
    Lowest-rated core beer, but Hefeweizen's banana/clove character is universally likeable. An underrated food pairing with spiced Indian starters.
    Untappd 3.14 · 79 ratings likely current
  9. 9
    Hoppy Belgian Blonde 6.2%
    No Untappd data found, but Belgian Blonde is inherently crowd-pleasing — fruity esters, gentle bitterness, clean finish. Confirm it's on tap tonight.
    beer roster — no Untappd data confirm availability
  10. 10
    Mango Ale seasonal
    If tapped: an Indian-inflected ale that leans into mango season. Worth a single pour for novelty — exactly the kind of locally-rooted expression a Bangalore brewpub does well.
    beer roster — seasonal ask if available

Section 7 · Heads-Up

What to Skip & Watch For

Honestly-sourced cautions — tagged reviewer-reported (≥2 independent diners) or our read (inference). Positive counter-signals included where found.
  • ⚠
    Volume — nightclub-level noise on weekends
    Two independent TripAdvisor reviewers in 2024 called the music deafening — one described it as "impossible to sit through dinner with family" with physical vibration. The venue doubles as a club with a dance floor and karaoke rooms. For quiet conversation or a family dinner, a weeknight is the safer call.
    reviewer-reported · Michelle R, TripAdvisor Jan 2024 reviewer-reported · Lizy J, TripAdvisor Mar 2024 comes with the club format
  • ℹ
    Beer — use the sampler, don't order blind
    The brewery's Untappd overall is 3.27 / 5 (609 ratings) — decent, not outstanding. The six-pour sampler flight (§1 #1) lets you taste before committing to a pint. The Helles Lager and The Bard (Hefeweizen) sit at the lower end of the tap list; Ale Othello (IPA) is the highest-rated. If you don't like hoppy beers, ask the bartender for a rec rather than guessing.
    Untappd · brewery overall 3.27 / 5 · 609 ratings our read · weaker styles: Helles, Hefeweizen
  • ℹ
    Cocktails — 100+ options, no independent benchmarks
    ShakesBierre markets over 100 cocktails but no independent reviewer has called out a specific cocktail as a must-order. That's a blind-order risk, not necessarily a quality flag. Ask the bartender what's good that night rather than picking by menu name.
    our read · no verifiable top picks found across platforms
  • ℹ
    Food — solid brewpub fare, not destination dining
    Even reviewers giving 5 stars describe the food as "okay, not exceptional, but good enough for a casual outing." One Dec 2023 reviewer specifically said starters were "not that good" — a thin single data point, not a pattern, but worth noting. The §2–§6 ranked picks are the reliably praised items; order from those and you'll eat well. Don't come here primarily for the food.
    reviewer-reported · Jathin H, TripAdvisor Dec 2023 (starters) our read · Sandeep Gunjan, Restaurant-Guru Apr 2026
  • ℹ
    Service — consistently praised; no recurring complaints found
    Google Maps flags "polite staff" in 142 separate reviews. TripAdvisor and Restaurant-Guru reviewers independently praise staff by name. No recurring service failure appeared across the 7,637-review dataset. This is a genuine positive: if something with the food or drinks is off, the staff tend to be responsive.
    Google Maps · polite staff ×142 mentions 4.5 ★ / 7,637 reviews · Google Maps Jun 2026
  • ℹ
    Billing — 10% service charge added before GST
    Standard for Brigade Road venues, but the gap between the menu price and the final bill is wider than it looks. Factor in the service charge when budgeting, especially on a round of craft beers and starters.
    our read · standard Bengaluru brewpub billing structure
  • ⚠
    SkyDeck (rooftop) — call ahead during monsoon season
    The open-air rooftop is a highlight, but Bengaluru's southwest monsoon runs June–September. No published rain policy has been found. If you're visiting specifically for the outdoor experience, call ahead (+91 96069 79931) to confirm the deck is open. A mid-evening shower means indoor reseating with little control over placement.
    our read · Bengaluru SW-monsoon climatology June – September risk
  • ℹ
    Weekend walk-ins — book a table in advance
    Brigade Road is one of Bengaluru's busiest nightlife corridors. With 7,600+ reviews and a club floor, walk-in availability on Friday and Saturday evenings is not guaranteed — especially if you want the rooftop or a specific booth.
    our read · Brigade Road weekend volume
