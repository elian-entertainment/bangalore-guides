☁ Before You Go — Check These

  • SkyDeck is fully open-air. Mid-June is monsoon onset in Bengaluru — evening showers are common. Ask when calling: "Is the SkyDeck open tonight, or do we go to the Theatre Floor if it rains?"
  • No published cover charge for a regular group of 5. A 10% service charge + GST applies on the bill. Confirm "any minimum spend tonight?" when phoning.
  • Reservations strongly advised on Saturday nights — the venue advises booking ahead on weekends. If rain closes the SkyDeck, the indoor floors fill faster.
  • Happy hours 12–5 PM — BOGO on house beers (official website). If you arrive early, use it.
