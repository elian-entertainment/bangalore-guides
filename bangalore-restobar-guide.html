🍸 The Cocktail Shortlist — Ranked

🥇 SOL55 Community Bar 📍 Koramangala 4th Block 🌿 Retractable roof · Live music
☔ Rain-safe — retractable roof closes when it rains

Opened in 2026, SOL55 is Koramangala's community cocktail bar — warm, earthy tones, exposed wood, and a retractable roof that feels like a garden when open and a cosy den when the monsoon forces it shut. Live music every Friday evening. The bartenders here are not pouring from a formula; they are writing one.

🍸 Signature Cocktails

  • Filter Coffee Negroni — Campari and gin twisted with South Indian filter coffee. Bitter, aromatic, unmistakably Bangalore.
  • Coconut Curry Colada — Rum with coconut and a curry-leaf whisper. Tastes like the coast.
  • Smoked Rosemary — Smoky, herbaceous, stirred. The one to order if you like Scotch-adjacent builds.
  • Berry I Need — Berry-forward and fresh. Crowd-pleaser.
  • Wildside — The bolder, spicier option on the menu.

🌿 Ask for a non-alcoholic version of the Coconut Curry Colada or the Berry I Need — both translate well as mocktails

🥈 The Permit Room 📍 MG Road (Magrath Rd) 🏛 Dravidian-themed · 4 floors · Rooftop
☔ Rain-safe — multiple indoor floors + covered rooftop terrace

Four floors of gloriously kitsch South Indian nostalgia — matchbox collages, satirical taxidermy, folk art posters, and an open rooftop terrace wrapped in greenery. The cocktail menu reads like a love letter to Dravidian culture; the food menu reads like your grandmother's recipe book interpreted by someone who grew up in a bar. Nothing in the MG Road belt tastes like this or looks like this.

🍸 Signature Cocktails

  • Paati's Magic Rasam — Whiskey, curry leaves, green chilli, rasam spice. The most Bangalore cocktail in existence.
  • Fizzy Kosambari — Gin with cucumber, coconut, and pomegranate. Fresh, floral, and summery.
  • Ginchax — Gin-based South Indian-inspired cooler. Easy to drink, hard to stop.
  • Kattanchaya — A cocktail built around filter coffee and dark spirits. For those who want dessert in a glass.

🌿 Ask for a Virgin Kosambari (cucumber-coconut-pomegranate sans gin) — it's refreshing and the kitchen makes it well

🥉 The Scene — Craft Kitchen & Bar 📍 JP Nagar 1st Phase 🌳 Nature-inspired · Trees & fairy lights
⚠️ Rain-watch — alfresco seating; indoor + mezzanine available as backup

The entire bar is built around living trees — exposed-to-elements, alfresco seating under cane flower-shaped lights with fairy lights threading the canopy. High ceilings, a mezzanine for intimate seating, and green hues everywhere. It is the most garden-like bar in JP Nagar without calling itself a garden. Hitaishi was right to send this one.

🍸 Signature Cocktails

  • Touching's Cocktail — Tequila, pickled fig, beetroot juice. Adventurous and unusual — the one to order if you like something you've never tasted before.
  • Clear Scenario — House signature. Clarified and clean. Consistently praised by reviewers.
  • Highest Hoovu — "Hoovu" means flower in Kannada — floral, aromatic, and distinctly South Bangalore.
  • Silk Smitha & Rom Com — Two more from their creative roster. Ask the bartender which is on that evening.

🌿 Ask for a mocktail version of Highest Hoovu — the floral-aromatic profile translates well without alcohol

4️⃣ The 13th Floor 📍 MG Road, Barton Centre 🌆 Iconic rooftop · Panoramic city views
⚠️ Rain-watch — open rooftop; partial glass cover from the building structure

The 13th Floor is where Bangalore came to drink before craft cocktails existed. Thirteen floors up in Barton Centre, MG Road, it offers an unobstructed 360-degree view of the city at night — glass and brushed steel, the city's twinkling grid below, aircraft lights overhead. The cocktail list is classical rather than creative, but the view does half the work. A place every Bangalorean should visit at least once.

🍸 Signature Cocktails

  • Wine & Champagne Cocktails — Their strongest suit. Kir Royale, sparkling sangria, and seasonal champagne builds.
  • Bangalore Sunset — House classic. Gin-based, citrus, grenadine. Best ordered at 6:30 PM when the city lights up.
  • Long Island Iced Tea — Reliably well-made. A workhorse in their programme.
  • Classic Martini variations — Stirred properly. Good if you like clean spirit-forward drinks.

🌿 Mocktail Bangalore Sunset (virgin version with soda + citrus) is available — ask the bar team

5️⃣ FLO 📍 Church Street 🏙 Modern fine-dining bar · Fully indoor
☔ Rain-proof — fully indoor venue

FLO sits in the thick of Church Street's bar-hopping strip — modern, contemporary, and far calmer inside than the street outside suggests. The cocktail menu skews international; the food covers Asian, North Indian, Continental, and Sushi. It is not the most distinctive place on this list, but it is the most centrally located and rain-proof option in the CBD, with easy access to the rest of Church Street if you want to make a night of it.

🍸 Cocktails

  • Curated cocktail menu — International classics and in-house signature builds. Full cocktail programme, not just LIIT and beer.
  • Wine & spirits selection — Strong spirits list — good if someone in your group prefers Scotch or wine over cocktails.
  • Sushi cocktail pairings — If you order the sushi, ask the bar team for their pairing recommendation.

🌿 Full mocktail menu available — ask for fresh-fruit based options

🤝 Hitaishi's Picks — Vetted

Your friend recommended six venues. Here's the honest verdict on each, checked against your areas and the cocktail-first criterion.

✅ The Scene (JP Nagar) — Verified & promoted into the main ranking at #3. Nature-inspired bar built around trees, creative named cocktails (Touching's Cocktail, Clear Scenario), fairy-light canopy. Good call, Hitaishi.
🔶 Ironhill — Main outlet: Marathahalli (outside your areas). Nearest in-area option: Forum South Mall, Konanukunte Cross (JP Nagar 7th Phase). Ironhill is the world's largest microbrewery — sheer scale is the spectacle (50,000 sq ft at Forum South). However, it is a beer-first brewery; the cocktail programme exists but is incidental. On a cocktail-focused family evening, it is outclassed by The Scene which is better located, better on cocktails, and more intimate. Worth a dedicated beer night another time.
❌ Outside your six areas — skipped:
  • Byg Brewski — Sarjapur Road / Hennur / Yeshwantpur. The lakeside campus is spectacular but none of these are in your zone. Best garden brewery in Bangalore for another day.
  • Maize & Malt — Whitefield (also KR Puram). 60,000 sq ft maze-like brewery. Far east. Outside area.
  • Long Boat — Marathahalli. Viking-themed brewery with a 30-ft waterfall. Outside area.
  • ICB (Indian Craft Brewery) — Nagawara / Manyata Tech Park. 1,500-seater, heritage-brews concept. North Bangalore. Outside area.

In a hundred years of eating and drinking in this city, I have rarely seen the cocktail and the food earn equal standing at the same table. SOL55 does it — a Filter Coffee Negroni in one hand and a Goan thali arriving at the table while the music starts. That is the Friday evening you came here for.

If the food argument matters as much as the cocktail argument, go to The Permit Room — order the Paati's Magic Rasam and the Pandi Spare Ribs, and tell me afterwards that anything on MG Road compares.

And if you want to show Hitaishi you listened — The Scene in JP Nagar is the pick from her list that earned its place. Trees overhead, fairy lights, a tequila-pickled-fig cocktail. She was right.

Call before you leave. It is Friday. The good tables go first.
