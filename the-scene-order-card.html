📌 Before You Go

  • Order everything early. Reviewers' #1 warning: Friday service slows badly after the crowd lands. Your 6 PM slot is the advantage — place starters and the main together.
  • Ask for the mezzanine — cosier, semi-private, and away from the street side (traffic noise at the front tables).
  • Valet only (free) — self-parking is a struggle here.
  • Live music on Fridays — call ahead to ask tonight's act and request a table away from the speakers if you two want to talk.
  • On the way out: the Amaré patisserie counter is at the entrance — macarons and cookies from ₹90. Take a box home for dad and your sister.
